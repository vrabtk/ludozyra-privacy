LudoZyra Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

LudoZyra ("the game", "we") is a Ludo board game for Android. This policy explains what information the game uses and what it does not. The game is published by Hari Krishna. Contact: a.harikrishna108@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your phone

To work, the game keeps a few things on your device only:

  • a random guest ID and the display name and avatar you choose;
  • your game settings (sound, music, vibration, board theme, language);
  • your match statistics and any match you saved to resume;
  • whether you bought "Remove ads".

This information stays on your device. The game does not have accounts, does not ask for your name, email or phone number, and does not send this information to our servers. You can erase it at any time from Settings → Delete local data, or by uninstalling the game.

2. Advertising

The free version shows ads using Google AdMob. To show ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. We ask for ad content suitable for all ages.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice later where the tool offers it.
  • You can reset or turn off personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Learn more about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you buy "Remove ads", the game stops showing ads and does not start the ad service.

3. Purchases

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment details; we never see your card or account information. We only learn that the purchase was made, so the game can switch ads off. Coins and rewards in the game, if any, are virtual items with no cash value and cannot be bought, sold or transferred.

4. Optional usage and crash reports

When you first open the game you can choose to allow anonymous usage and crash reports. This choice is optional and you can withdraw it in Settings. At the time of writing, the game does not send these reports to any server.

5. Children

LudoZyra is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete all game data on your device from Settings, or by uninstalling.
  • Control ad personalisation through the consent prompt and your Android settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we do not hold personal data about you on our servers, there is nothing for us to retrieve; for data held by Google, use your Google account's privacy controls.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change is significant, the game will tell you.
