LudoZyra Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-21

1. Who we are

LudoZyra ("the game", "we", "us") is a Ludo board game for Android published by Hari Krishna A, an individual developer in India. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. Contact: a.harikrishna108@gmail.com.

2. Information kept on your device

  • a random guest ID, and the display name and avatar (or the photo you choose) shown in the game. The game has no accounts and never asks for your real name, email address or phone number;
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, board theme, language), your match counters (matches played and won) and any match you saved to resume;
  • your coin balance, the coins you earned from the hourly reward and rewarded ads, premium chat emojis you unlocked, and the times these rewards were last claimed;
  • whether you bought "Remove ads".

This information stays on your device. We do not receive it, and we cannot recover it for you if you delete the game or switch phones.

3. Your profile photo

If you choose "Upload a photo", the game reads only the one picture you select (through the Android photo picker, so the game gets no access to the rest of your gallery). It is reduced to a small square image and kept on your device. It is never uploaded by the game.

4. Chat

Chat in a match uses ready-made messages and emojis only; you cannot type free text. Messages are shown on your screen and are not sent anywhere, and no chat history is kept.

5. Advertising and third-party partners

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the main menu, full-screen ads before a game, after a game and when you save and leave a game, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for coins. Ads are never shown while you roll the dice or move a pawn.

To show ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and your interactions with ads. We ask for ad content suitable for all ages.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised, using Google's consent tool.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Rewarded ads pay coins only after the ad network confirms you watched the ad. If you buy "Remove ads" the banner and full-screen ads stop; rewarded ads stay available because you start them yourself.

6. Coins, rewards and purchases

Coins are virtual items with no cash value. They cannot be bought, sold, transferred or exchanged for money or prizes, and they stay on your device. "Remove ads" is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment details; we never see your card or account information, only the confirmation that the purchase was made.

7. Usage and crash reports

When you first open the game you can allow anonymous usage and crash reports. This is optional and you can withdraw it in Settings at any time. At present the game keeps these diagnostics on your device only and does not send them to any server.

8. Storage, security and retention

Your guest ID is kept in the Android secure storage; other data is kept in the game's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. We keep the data until you delete it (Settings → Delete local data) or uninstall the game. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google AdMob, Google's consent tool and Google Play process as described above, under Google's own policies.

9. Sharing

We do not sell your information and we do not hold any personal information about you that we could share. Information processed by Google (advertising and purchases) is shared with Google and its partners as described in sections 5 and 6.

10. Children

LudoZyra is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to content suitable for all audiences. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

11. Your rights and choices

  • Delete everything the game keeps on your device with Settings → Delete local data, or by uninstalling the game.
  • Change your ad personalisation choice in the consent prompt and in your Android settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the GDPR or similar laws) you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal data and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data about you on our servers there is nothing for us to retrieve; for data held by Google, use your Google account's privacy controls. You may also write to us with any request.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and the game asks for your acceptance again when a change is significant. Questions or requests: a.harikrishna108@gmail.com.
